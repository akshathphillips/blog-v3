You were supposed to be falling asleep.

We're in Richmond this week for your maasis' wedding, Jotie and Jessica, and the afternoon caught up with you the way it does to little kids. So the two of us went to lie down for your nap on a twin mattress, the small kind, which meant we were cozy whether we planned to be or not.

I was reading you *Goodnight Moon*. You had your milk. And somewhere in the middle of the great green room, you stopped drinking, took the bottle out of your mouth, and looked right at me.

"Love you, papa."

Then a lot of kisses. Not one polite kiss; lots of them, the way you do it, like you'd been saving them up.

I don't think you'll remember this afternoon. You're too small, and it was too ordinary: a nap, a book, a borrowed mattress in Nitin mamu's home. But I want it written down, because you said it first, without being asked, and you meant it enough to stop drinking your milk to say it.

We lay back down. I read you a few more books. Your eyes got heavy and then they closed, and you fell asleep against me, and I stayed there a while longer than I needed to.

Love you too, Avi. More than the books can hold.

Papa

Starting a training log, mostly to keep myself honest. Motivation is a liar; a record isn't. If I write down what I actually did each week, future me can't pretend the work happened when it didn't — and can't forget it when it did.

## Why

Two reasons, and they're both at home. I want to be strong for my ladies, and I want to still be the dad who can keep up with Avi twenty years from now. Somewhere in there is a number — working toward ~20% body fat by my 35th birthday — but the number is just a proxy for the real goal: a body that holds up.

## The plan, kept boring

- **Lift 3–4x a week.** Compound-first — squat, hinge, press, pull. Boring on purpose.
- **Progressive overload.** A little more than last week, most weeks. The graph only has to trend up.
- **Eat like I mean it.** Enough protein, mostly real food, no heroics.
- **Sleep is training too.** The toddler has opinions about this one.

## Baseline

This is the honest part — future weeks measure against here. I'll log the real numbers as I go: bodyweight, the big lifts, how the week actually felt. Week 1 is just planting the flag.

## Next week

Show up four times. Log every session. That's the whole assignment.

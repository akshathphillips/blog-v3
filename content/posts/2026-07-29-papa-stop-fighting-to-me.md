Avi,

You have a thing for cupcakes with icing, specifically for eating them in the car on the way to school. By your logic it's the perfect breakfast: sweet, portable, and best enjoyed in motion.

It had been a while since we'd had them around. So on Wednesday, we were already heading out (you were nearly in your car seat) when you remembered there was icing cake in the house. This could not stand. Back inside we went.

Then I made the mistake of being reasonable. I offered to cut it in half: some for now, some for later. You did not care for this proposal. There was negotiation. There were terms. There was, briefly, a full summit over the future of a single cupcake.

And somewhere in the middle of it, worn out with me, you looked up and said:

"Papa, stop fighting to me."

I knew what you meant: *stop arguing with me.* But my heart just filled right up. Because not so long ago you couldn't have told me off if you'd tried, and here you were, holding your ground, giving me sass, fighting your corner over frosting.

You won, of course. You usually do.

Fight away, little one. I'm on your side even when I'm on the other end of the cupcake.

Papa

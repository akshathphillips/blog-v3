The last thing I wrote here was in early August. Then the site went quiet.

When I started this version of the site, I wrote that the first two died because the writing never happened. I'd rather this one didn't go the same way. A few quiet months is a pause, not an ending.

I finally have some time for myself again, so I'm coming back to this. The training log starts over on Monday with a fresh Week 1, and the letters to Avi pick back up too.

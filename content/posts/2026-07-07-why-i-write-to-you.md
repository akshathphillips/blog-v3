Avi,

This corner of the website is yours. Everything here is a letter from me to you — written whenever something happens that I don't want to trust to memory, because memory is a lousy archivist and you deserve better records.

One day you'll be old enough to read these, and I'll be a slightly different dad than the one writing this. That's the trick of it: I can't send anything forward in time except words, so words are what you'll get. Some letters will be about big days. Most will be about small ones, because I suspect the small ones are the ones that turn out to matter.

I love you. More letters soon.

— Dad
